## Settling-in care

Use bright indirect light and keep the glass out of direct sun and away from
heat sources. Check the actual medium and look for standing water before
adding any: flattened foliage after shipping is not enough evidence to water.
A closed terrarium retains moisture, so avoid a fixed watering calendar or a
cactus dry-down rule. These starting precautions follow
[RHS terrarium guidance](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/types/houseplants/bottle-gardens-and-terrariums).

For the nearby grow light, a modest move farther away is a cautious trial, not a
required correction established by the 9,000-lux reading. Compare readings at
the leaves inside, including the brightest and shaded sides, and check for
warming during the light cycle. The glass, cork, foliage, and measurement angle
make the outside reading an incomplete description of the plants' exposure.
Record any actual move and follow-up reading rather than treating 7,000 lux as
a safety cutoff.

Some condensation is expected in a closed terrarium. If it stays heavily fogged
or dripping, briefly remove the lid or wipe the glass and reassess; RHS advises
venting excess condensation. There is no prescribed daily venting schedule and
no need to chase the seller's exact 90% suggestion from one reading. Check the
sensor's model and placement before relying on its behavior near saturation.

Remove clearly dead loose leaves or decaying debris carefully. Do not strip
the brown moss or pull living stems apart merely because they look untidy.
Compare a later photograph with this arrival record for changes in posture,
leaf condition, and new growth. Recovery time is unknown. No fertilizer dose,
water volume, humidity target, or automatic daily Check task is assigned.

Record future measured humidity as one **Humidity** event for **P38 / #12** in
**% RH**, with the actual reading time and sensor location.
