## Research behind the grouping

- **Higher-light priority (H):** the RHS places rainbow hedgehog cactus in full
  sun, and Singapore NParks gives the same preference for Old Man of the Andes.
  These support prioritizing `C2` and `B2` for stronger usable exposure after
  acclimation. [RHS rainbow cactus](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/115504/echinocereus-rigidissimus/details),
  [NParks _Oreocereus trollii_](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/6/1/6142).
- **Bright rosettes:** NC State recommends sunny conditions for _Echeveria_.
  _Sempervivum_ benefits from sun but also cooler conditions and is less suited
  to persistent heat. Put `H1` and `H2` in bright, unobstructed positions;
  "higher light" does not mean the hottest or closest possible position.
  The existing indoor-only placement choice for `H1` is retained, with its
  cool-season needs still relevant. [NC State _Echeveria_](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/echeveria/),
  [NC State _Sempervivum_](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/sempervivum/).
- **Monk's hood needs a qualification:** RHS lists full sun outdoors but bright
  filtered light under glass. `C3` belongs in a bright group; its label is not
  permission to move it abruptly into an unmeasured hotspot.
  [RHS _Astrophytum ornatum_](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/1880/astrophytum-ornatum/details).
- **Split rock is not a shade plant:** SANBI recommends a sunny windowsill for
  _Pleiospilos nelii_, and the actual Royal Flush grower listing warns against
  low light. `G3` keeps a bright, open position. Its leaf-replacement cycle
  remains separate from cactus watering; the layout does not set a schedule.
  [SANBI split rock](https://pza.sanbi.org/pleiospilos-nelii),
  [Mountain Crest Gardens Royal Flush care](https://mountaincrestgardens.com/pleiospilos-nelii-royal-flush-split-rock/).
- **Gentler starting positions (G):** `A1` and `G1` are variegated chin cacti.
  NC State's moon-cactus article favors bright indirect light but focuses
  heavily on grafted plants; this collection's plants must not be relabeled as
  chlorophyll-free grafts. The Black Widow grower also lists full sun, so the
  wall-side choice is a cautious starting inference for this reflected-light
  setup, not evidence that the cultivar requires shade.
  [NC State _Gymnocalycium mihanovichii_](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/gymnocalycium-mihanovichii/),
  [Mountain Crest Gardens Black Widow](https://mountaincrestgardens.com/gymnocalycium-mihanovichii-f-variegata-black-widow-wfzy/).
- **Money tree:** put `#3` at the glass-table periphery, with filtered window
  light and its leaves clear of the strongest lamp exposure. NC State describes
  indirect light for _Pachira aquatica_; that is a genus-level care comparison
  here, not proof of the collection's tentative _Pachira_ cf. _glabra_ ID.
  [NC State money tree](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/pachira-aquatica/).

Other placements combine the existing [individual profiles](../plants/), plant

## Check the arrangement before settling it

1. Dry-fit the existing pots on the actual tops, without allowing rims to hang
   over the edge. Check the table rotation before adopting the four-by-six map.
2. Compare light at each plant's actual top and at the low rosettes, with the
   return panel in place. Repeat during a direct window beam if one occurs.
   A table-surface measurement cannot represent every canopy height.
3. Keep the reported light program steady during the placement trial. Do not
   also add risers or increase power, which would make the response harder to
   interpret. The drawings do not change controller settings.
4. Watch new growth for stretching, and exposed tissue for bleaching or
   persistent stress after a move. Change one placement at a time. Assess root
   dryness and plant condition separately from the light group when watering.
