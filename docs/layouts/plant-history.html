How to add a reading

Notes appear on this public page. Keep private details and precise home-location information out of the sheet.

  1. Open Quick log and find the permanent row for this plant. The P-number remains stable even if its physical pot label changes.
  2. Fill only what you checked. Weight, height, width, watering, and condition do not need to happen together.
  3. For weights, use Dry immediately before a soak, Wet after runoff has stopped, and Routine for an in-between reading.
  4. Tick Save. The logger appends one or more event-specific History rows and clears the input fields; older readings are never overwritten.
  5. In the mobile logger, Repot records the old and new pot size and advances Pot setup automatically. Setup 1 means the first tracked whole-pot configuration—not a 1-inch pot.
