# Grow-spot diagrams

Open [indoor-acclimation-calendar.html](./indoor-acclimation-calendar.html) for
the browser/print calendar covering the July 30 preparation, August 2 RO-water
move-in, August 3 light start, weekly adjustments and rotations, manual
temperature/RH readings, natural daylight context, and September 21 seasonal
timer.

Open [grow-spot-layout.html](./grow-spot-layout.html) in a browser for the
current room, table, plant-position, height, fan, camera, and riser diagrams.
Its four views are tabs, and its theme choice is shared with the calendar. In
the plant-position view, hover, tap, or keyboard-focus any pot or shared planter
to reveal its common and botanical names, collection record, origin, placement,
identification status, and field-guide link.

The page reflects the current working arrangement:

- the occupied bay is 36 in wide by about 60 in long;
- both tabletops are 18 in high;
- the two shared planters stay on the 24 in round glass table at the window end;
- all 12 individually potted starter plants use the 16 × 13 in wooden table by
  the room end;
- the tables run end to end along the approximately 60 in bay, with their
  centerlines beneath the window-to-room long axis of the AW200SE;
- the 24 in round diameter plus the 16 in depth of the wooden table occupies
  about 40 in of that length, leaving about 20 in to distribute around and
  between the tables using the real physical fit;
- the AeroWave stays on the divider pole and blows diagonally across both
  tables; and
- the GrowCam stays on the right wall and frames both tables.

Every diagram labels its viewpoint. In the top-down view, the window is at the
top, the room is at the bottom, the divider and fan are on the left, and the
white wall and camera are on the right. The round table comes first at the
window; the wooden table follows lengthwise toward the room.

The 2026-07-31 tape photos are indexed in
[`assets/measurements`](../../assets/measurements/README.md). Their starter and
shared-planter height bands inform the riser calculator. The wooden-table
diagram shows the measured three-column by four-row fit and a photo-derived,
height-first starting assignment for the four riser shelves. The photos do not
provide 12 exact tabletop-to-tip readings, so remeasure each plant directly
before treating the assignments as final.
