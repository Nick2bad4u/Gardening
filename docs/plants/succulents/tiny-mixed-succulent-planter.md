## Why the component names stay broad

Echeveria includes many species and cultivated hybrids, while the two
paddle-kalanchoe species are often confused in the nursery trade. Flowers and
mature growth offer useful distinguishing features. The component pages
explain those comparisons alongside each group's growth habit.

## Rotation and watch points

- Photograph the same side with `#6` visible so component loss, stretching, and
  crowding are easy to compare.
- The pale rosettes may carry protective farina; avoid handling or wiping it
  merely to improve appearance.
- Red or orange coloration can change with light and temperature. Treat a rapid
  shift together with texture and growth, not color alone, as evidence.
- If one plant rots, isolate the affected component and inspect adjoining roots;
  do not assume the whole planter needs more water.
- Check sheltered growth for mealybugs and scale. Inspect each foliage group
  before choosing a treatment; do not mistake Echeveria farina or the
  kalanchoe's natural surface bloom for an infestation.
