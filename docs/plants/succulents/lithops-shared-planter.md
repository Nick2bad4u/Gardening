## Watch points

- Record leaf stage, firmness, basal condition, and pests before interpreting
  wrinkles as thirst. A soft or translucent base needs inspection.
- Keep old-leaf absorption distinct from active growth needing water; do not
  use a calendar interval or water because another succulent is ready.
- Save labels and clear top/side photographs before revising the identity.
