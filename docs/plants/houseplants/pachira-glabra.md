## Origin, form, and publication

For the working species candidate, Kew accepts _Pachira glabra_, first published
in 1868, and places its native range in southeastern and southern Brazil. It is
a wet-tropical tree, not a succulent, so it should not follow the cactus
dry-down schedule.

## Rotation, propagation, and watch points

- Rotate the pot 90° weekly if it leans toward the window.
- Watch new leaves for bleaching, crispy margins, persistent droop, or rapid
  yellowing after the transplant.
- Empty the saucer after watering. A larger plastic pot dries more slowly than
  the 4-inch nursery pot.
- Stem cuttings and seed are possible, but stable post-transplant growth is
  the priority now.
