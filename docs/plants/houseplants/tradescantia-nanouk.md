## Watch points

- New crispy edges can warrant reviewing drying and exposure; they do not prove one cause by themselves.
- Keep the drainage opening clear of the table support and any runoff; “no saucer” is not a substitute for a clear outlet.
- If shoots become long and bare, assess light and growth habit before changing water frequency. Occasional pinching is an option for a fuller plant, not a required daily task.
- Keep the plant out of reach of pets that chew plants; Costa Farms states that Nanouk is not intended for human or animal consumption.
