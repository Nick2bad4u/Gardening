# Rose pincushion cactus

- Inventory: Cactus-06 — _Mammillaria zeilmanniana_
- Label ID: `F1`
- Identification: **high confidence**
- Acquired from: Carlsons' Greenhouse, Torrey Road, Fenton, Michigan; exact date not recorded
- Current pot: 4-inch individual pot
- Photo archive: [licensed species-reference images](../../../assets/plants/mammillaria-zeilmanniana/)

## Names and identity

| Kind                    | Name                                           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Accepted botanical name | _Mammillaria zeilmanniana_                     |
| Common name             | Rose pincushion cactus                         |
| Diagnostic combination  | Fine white radials plus reddish central spines |

The collection identification is supported by approximately 15–18 white
radial spines and four reddish central spines, three straight and one hooked.
Rosy-purple flowers commonly make a complete ring around the upper stem.

## Origin, form, and publication

Kew accepts the species, first published in 1931, and places its native range
in Guanajuato and Querétaro, central Mexico. It grows in desert and dry
shrubland.

## Care in this collection

| Topic               | Practical approach                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Grow-light position | Bright middle zone after acclimation; use flower and new-spine development as useful response signals. |
| Water               | Soak, drain, and allow a complete dry-down. Avoid leaving a cool crown wet.                            |
| Pot and mix         | The 4-inch pot is suitable until offsets or roots crowd it; keep the body above the top dressing.      |
| Flowers             | A dry, cooler rest followed by active growth can support the characteristic flower ring.               |

## Rotation, propagation, and watch points

- Rotate 90° clockwise during the normal Sunday rotation.
- Offsets and seed are practical propagation routes.
- Hooked central spines catch fabric and skin; move the pot by its rim.
- Check the axils and crown for mealybugs before assuming new white material
  is normal wool.
