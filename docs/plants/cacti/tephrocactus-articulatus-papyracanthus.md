## Origin, form, and taxonomy

_Tephrocactus articulatus_ is an Argentine cactus with detachable, rounded to
ovoid stem segments. Mountain Crest describes the papery-spined form as a
stacking, branching plant whose segments detach and reroot easily.

GBIF accepts _T. articulatus_ at species rank and treats var.
_papyracanthus_ as a synonym under that species. The variety name remains
useful horticultural evidence and should not be silently removed from the
seller record.

## Care in this collection

| Topic               | Practical starting approach                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrival             | Unpack over a tray because loose segments may already have detached; inspect the base and roots before watering.    |
| Grow-light position | Use a bright cactus zone after acclimation, but begin outside the hottest center until the shipped surface adjusts. |
| Water               | Water only after arrival condition, roots, medium dryness, and pot weight support it; then soak, drain, and re-dry. |
| Pot and mix         | Favor a stable, freely draining setup; a top-heavy chain of segments can lever itself out of a light pot.           |
| Handling            | Support the lowest segment instead of grabbing the paper spines or upper joints.                                    |

## Rotation, propagation, and watch points

- Begin 90° weekly rotation only after its `G2` label orientation and stable
  base are recorded.
- A detached healthy segment can be allowed to callus and rooted, but an
  arrival break should first be photographed as shipping evidence.
- Paper-like spines are still cactus spines, and small glochids or sharper
  structures may be less obvious than the broad ribbons.
- Do not bury lower stem segments deeply simply to compensate for an unstable
  pot; fix the container support instead.
