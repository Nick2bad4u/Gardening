## Proposed care — archived research

| Topic       | Practical starting approach                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Light       | Medium to bright indirect light, following Costa Farms' cultivar guidance. Use a gentler position than the cactus center; the proposed position and leaf-height reading remain unrecorded. The adjacent area estimate of 18–22 DLI is not automatically its target.       |
| Water       | Allow some upper-medium drying, then water and drain. Avoid the prolonged complete dry-down used for many cacti, while also avoiding persistently saturated roots. Learn any future planting separately rather than copying a cactus interval or grams-per-day threshold. |

Costa Farms recommends letting the top inch or two dry while avoiding both complete root-ball drying and soggy conditions. That wording describes its general houseplant context: this small pot and gritty mix need their own observed drying behavior, not a literal fixed-depth rule or a weekly calendar.

## Reference watch points

- New crispy edges can warrant reviewing drying and exposure; they do not prove one cause by themselves.
- Keep the drainage opening clear of the table support and any runoff; “no saucer” is not a substitute for a clear outlet.
- If shoots become long and bare, assess light and growth habit before changing water frequency. Occasional pinching is an option for a fuller plant, rather than a daily task.
- Keep the plant out of reach of pets that chew plants; Costa Farms states that Nanouk is not intended for human or animal consumption.
