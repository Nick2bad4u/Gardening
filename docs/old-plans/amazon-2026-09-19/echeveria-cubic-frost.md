| Breeder record   | Renee O'Connell, Vista, California; US plant patent PP27,527 |

The primary patent describes a cross made in 2011 and the selection of 'Cubic Frost' that year. Its pale, upturned leaves and tendency to make offsets support the intended cultivar description. They do not authenticate an unseen retail starter. The patent was published on January 3, 2017; that is a patent date, not a species-description date.

## Proposed care — archived research

| Topic              | Practical starting approach                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Light              | Clear, bright grow light. The owner reports approximately 18–22 estimated DLI in the intended far-table area; this is an app-derived area estimate, not a reading at this planned rosette. Confirm the final leaf-height exposure before treating it as a measured position. |
| Shared arrangement | Leave space for the rosette to widen. Keep the taller Coppertone and Ruby Slippers growth from shading its center. This was a proposed arrangement only.                                                                                                                     |
| Water              | Use the shared planter's actual dry-down and the collection's [watering strategy](../../watering-strategy.md). Do not water this component separately or borrow another pot's dry reference.                                                                                 |
| Pot and mix        | The owner selected Molly's Succulent Mix and an 8-inch drainage pot. Four 2-inch retail starters do not establish root volume or drying speed in that container. The arrangement was never planted.                                                                          |
| Handling           | Support the root ball and pot rather than using the curled leaves as handles. Keep dressing clear of the rosette center.                                                                                                                                                     |

NC State's genus guidance supports bright light, good drainage, and relatively low water demand. This is a practical succulent starting point, not a cultivar-specific PPFD limit or a fixed watering interval. The owner's continuous canopy and under-table airflow can change drying behavior; it does not supply a measured baseline.

## Reference watch points

- Compare new growth with the cultivar's naturally curled leaf shape before treating it as damage.
- Keep the rosette center open as taller companions develop.
- Separate plants if their growth or watering needs become incompatible in a future shared arrangement.
