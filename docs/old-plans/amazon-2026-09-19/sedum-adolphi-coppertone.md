## Reference watch points

The NParks species reference describes a low succulent subshrub with white flowers and notes that excess moisture can contribute to stem rot. Stems, leaf arrangement, and flowers would be useful identification features for a future specimen.

For this arrangement, crowding or a stretching stem is a reason to assess the affected component's position. It is not automatically evidence that all four plants need more water or brighter light.
