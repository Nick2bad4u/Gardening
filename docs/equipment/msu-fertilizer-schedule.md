# MSU fertilizer schedule

Last updated: 2026-08-26

This is the working nutrient plan for the collection's Tezula/Greencare **MSU
13-3-15 Tap/Rain/RO formula**. It is a low-dose starting plan for these actual
pots, the 60/40 Molly's-and-perlite cactus mix, RO water, the AW200SE, and the
indoor dry-down trial. It is not a fixed command to water on a calendar date.

## Short answer

- **Do not fertilize the cacti at every watering by default.** Alternate one
  dilute MSU watering with one plain-RO watering while compact new growth is
  visible. A warm room or a due watering does not, by itself, prove active
  growth.
- Use **0.75 g of MSU powder per US gallon of RO water** on a cactus feed day.
- Tomorrow, **August 27, use plain RO with no fertilizer** for the 18
  August-14 repots (`P01`-`P18`). If no soak was completed after the repot,
  this becomes their first recorded post-repot watering and establishes the
  clean pot-setup-2 wet-weight baseline.
- Do not add separate Cal-Mag, sulfur, Foliage-Pro, or another fertilizer to
  the same gallon. This MSU formula already supplies calcium, magnesium, and
  micronutrients.
- Water only a dry, ready pot. Fertilizer changes what is in an otherwise due
  watering; it is never a reason to water early.

If an individual cactus has a soft or dark base, a fresh open wound, newly cut
roots, or another possible rot sign, hold that pot out of the group watering
and inspect it first.

## Why the dose is lower than the package direction

Tezula lists this product as 13% nitrogen, 3% phosphate (`P2O5`), 15% potash
(`K2O`), 8% calcium, 2% water-soluble magnesium, and a complete set of listed
micronutrients. The product is explicitly formulated for RO, rain, or tap
water. Its retail directions give **one teaspoon per gallon**, and Tezula
suggests two or three fertilized applications followed by a plain-water root
drench.

That is a general product rate, not a cactus requirement. University extension
guidance consistently describes cacti and succulents as light feeders. UC
guidance calls for a low-nitrogen fertilizer at about one-quarter strength;
Minnesota Extension recommends only one or two cactus feedings during active
late-spring or summer growth. The collection uses a weak, measured compromise
because its medium is soilless and the plants remain under controlled light.

At **0.75 g per US gallon**, the declared analysis supplies approximately:

| Declared nutrient  | Approximate concentration |
| ------------------ | ------------------------: |
| Nitrogen           |                   26 mg/L |
| Phosphate (`P2O5`) |                    6 mg/L |
| Potash (`K2O`)     |                   30 mg/L |
| Calcium            |                   16 mg/L |
| Magnesium          |                    4 mg/L |

These are calculations from the guaranteed percentages and a 3.785 L US
gallon, not a meter reading. Product density can change how many grams fit in a
teaspoon, which is why the gram scale is the collection standard.

## First cycles after the August 14 repot

Use the watering event, dryness, and new growth—not an assumed seven-day
interval—to advance this sequence:

| Watering event             | Solution                   | Decision rule                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Aug. 27 planned first soak | Plain RO                   | Proceed for a firm, dry, ready pot. Record dry setup-2 weight first, water to runoff, drain 30-60 minutes, empty the saucer, and record wet weight. |
| Next ready watering        | **0.75 g MSU per gallon**  | Feed only if the plant completed the first dry-down normally, is making healthy compact growth, and has no root/base problem.                       |
| Following ready watering   | Plain RO                   | Water thoroughly enough for runoff; this is the salt-flush watering.                                                                                |
| Next ready watering        | **0.75 g MSU per gallon**  | Repeat only during visible active growth.                                                                                                           |
| Thereafter                 | Alternate MSU and plain RO | Stay at 0.75 g/gal if growth is compact and healthy. More fertilizer is not a goal.                                                                 |

Keep **0.75 g per gallon** as the normal cactus rate through at least two
successful feed cycles. **1.00 g per gallon** is an optional future ceiling,
not a scheduled increase. Consider it only if several cycles show compact
active growth, a normal dry-down, no mineral crust, and a reason to suspect the
lower dose is insufficient. It provides about 34 mg/L nitrogen, 21 mg/L
calcium, and 5 mg/L magnesium. More fertilizer is not a growth goal.

When growth slows with the shorter seasonal light program or cooler room
conditions, use plain RO and stop feeding. If the cacti continue producing
clearly healthy, compact new growth through winter under the AW200SE, a
conservative exception is **0.50-0.75 g/gal no more often than every third
ready watering**. Never fertilize a cool, resting cactus merely because the
calendar says a feeding is due.

## Measuring a batch

Use the 0.01 g pocket scale. The larger 0.5 g-resolution pot scale is not
precise enough for this dose.

| Finished solution | MSU powder at the 0.75 g/gal cactus rate |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------: |
| 1 US quart        |                                   0.19 g |
| 1/2 US gallon     |                                   0.38 g |
| 1 US gallon       |                                   0.75 g |
| 2 US gallons      |                                   1.50 g |
| 5 US gallons      |                                   3.75 g |

The five-gallon HDPE jug remains the plain-RO storage jug. Do not turn the
whole jug into fertilizer solution just because the math is convenient.

1. Use a clean, dry weighing cup; tare it on the 0.01 g scale.
2. Weigh the powder. Do not estimate a fraction of the included scoop.
3. Put about three-quarters of the final RO volume in the clean one-gallon
   pitcher.
4. Add the powder and stir until no granules remain. Top up to the final gallon
   mark and stir again.
5. Apply at room temperature in two slow passes a few minutes apart, continuing
   until the entire root ball is wet and solution exits the drainage holes.
6. Drain 30-60 minutes, empty every saucer, and discard any unused solution the
   same day. Rinse the pitcher and weighing cup with plain water.

For the Garden Logger, record `Nutrients: Yes`, product
`Tezula MSU 13-3-15`, amount `0.75 g/US gal`, and the actual observation date.
On a plain-water cycle, record `Nutrients: No`. A fertilizer entry does not
replace the Water and Wet-weight events.

## Other plants in the collection

Do not automatically pour the cactus batch into every pot:

| Plant or group                            | MSU starting rule                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `#1` shared rehab cactus planter          | Use plain RO for the next ready watering. If all three remaining plants are healthy and growing compactly, start at 0.50 g/gal no more often than every third ready watering.         |
| Shared succulent planter and Kiwi aeonium | 0.75 g/gal every second or third ready watering during compact active growth; plain RO otherwise.                                                                                     |
| Money tree                                | 1.00 g/gal every second ready watering during active leafy growth. Use either MSU or another complete fertilizer, never both in the same watering. Reduce or pause when growth slows. |
| Newly delivered Mountain Crest plants     | No arrival-day fertilizer. Keep the nursery medium and any seller-applied nutrients unknown until inspection; wait for establishment and at least one normal watering/dry-down cycle. |
| `Pleiospilos nelii` 'Royal Flush'         | Do not feed by the group calendar. Establish it first and follow the leaf-replacement and watering state; excess water is the larger immediate risk.                                  |
| Indoor `Sempervivum` 'Coconut Crystal'    | Begin with no fertilizer on arrival. If it remains compact and actively growing after establishment, use 0.50 g/gal only occasionally rather than the cactus alternation.             |
