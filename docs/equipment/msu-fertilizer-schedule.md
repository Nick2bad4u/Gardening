# MSU and Foliage-Pro nutrient guide

Last updated: 2026-11-27

This is the collection's working trial of **more frequent, dilute feeding**
with Tezula/Greencare **MSU 13-3-15 Tap/Rain/RO** and **SUPERthrive Foliage-Pro
9-3-6**. Choose the appropriate product only after the plant is ready for water
and suitable for feeding. The recipes are collection starting points for RO
water, the actual growing media, and indoor lights; they are not measured
nutrient requirements or treatments for unexplained yellowing.

For **established, healthy, actively growing plants**, use the matching recipe
at each otherwise-needed watering while feeding remains suitable. Check the
existing fertilizer charge and apply the plant exceptions below.

| Plant group                   | Mix per US gallon of RO                    |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Ordinary cacti and succulents | **0.50 g MSU**                             |
| Foliage houseplants           | **1/8 US tsp Foliage-Pro (about 0.62 mL)** |

This replaces the old default feed/plain-water alternation. Use plain RO when
feeding is unsuitable; there is no automatic fourth- or sixth-watering flush.
Read the [foliage plan](#foliage-pro-starting-plan),
[salt-management guidance](#plain-water-and-salt-management), and
[collection exceptions](#other-plants-in-the-collection) before including a pot.

The display uses the installed [AW200 + AeroLight 240 W pair](./aw200-and-aerolight-240w.md).
Follow growth and normal drying when reviewing the feeding rate.

### Foliage-Pro starting plan

Use **SUPERthrive Foliage-Pro 9-3-6 liquid fertilizer**, the product family already named in the collection's money-tree feeding history. This recommendation applies to a bottle marked **Foliage-Pro 9-3-6**; other SUPERthrive products require their own instructions.

Before starting or continuing regular feeding, check any known nursery or replacement-mix fertilizer information during normal care. If slow-release fertilizer is still supplying the pot, the mix has a fresh fertilizer charge, or establishment/feed suitability remains unclear, use **plain RO, no nutrients**, and reconsider at a later ordinary watering. Do not disturb roots or add daily checks to obtain feeding eligibility. These checks also apply to the money tree and the newer foliage plants; a purchase or repot date alone does not establish eligibility.

Once feeding is appropriate, eligible foliage plants can share this gentle starting recipe:

- **Dose:** **1/8 US teaspoon per US gallon of RO water** (approximately **0.62 mL per 3.785 L**).
- **Frequency:** use the dilute mix at **each otherwise-needed watering** while healthy new growth and feeding suitability continue. Each pot follows its own drying target, even when plants share a batch. This is a collection trial, not a manufacturer requirement or a claim that all foliage pots dry at the same rate.
- **Fallback:** use plain RO when growth pauses, roots or stems are stressed, mineral buildup is evident, or the fertilizer charge remains a concern. Resume only when feeding is appropriate; do not make up a missed feed or increase the dose automatically.
- **Watering remains separate:** #7 needs partial drying; #8 needs its upper 1–2 inches to dry. Do not water early to feed, wait for a cactus plateau, or request extra weights to maintain this plan.

The original Dyna-Gro manufacturer data sheet gives **1/4 teaspoon per gallon with each watering** as a maintenance option. This trial uses half that concentration. At comparable watering volumes, 1/8 tsp at each watering supplies the same total product as the previous collection plan of 1/4 tsp every second watering. The current bottle label has not been inspected; follow its product-specific restrictions. RHS supports dilute feeding during active Peperomia growth, but neither source establishes this collection dose as optimal.

| Water volume  | Foliage-Pro at 1/8 US teaspoon per US gallon |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------: |
| 1 US gallon   |                            1/8 tsp ≈ 0.62 mL |
| 1/2 US gallon |                           1/16 tsp ≈ 0.31 mL |
| 1 litre       |                                    ≈ 0.16 mL |

Measure the liquid by volume with a graduated syringe or measuring spoon. Its density has not been measured, so these are not gram doses. Add it to the measured water and mix only the amount needed for the session. Keep the stored RO jug plain. Use Foliage-Pro as the only complete fertilizer in that batch; do not add MSU, separate Cal-Mag, sulfur, or another complete feed. Wet the mix evenly at an otherwise-ready watering, let it drain, and empty retained runoff. Extra flushing is not part of this new-plant plan.

When a feed is actually completed, record the Water event with `Nutrients: Yes`, product `SUPERthrive Foliage-Pro 9-3-6`, and amount `1/8 tsp/US gal`. Count any other complete fertilizer as a feed too. An unknown nursery feed remains unknown, and a recommendation is never a completed event. The owner's lower weighing priority for #7/#8 remains unchanged; a convenient weight during real care is optional, not a condition of feeding.

## Short answer

- **For eligible ordinary cacti and succulents, trial 0.50 g of MSU powder per
  US gallon of RO water at each otherwise-ready watering.** Healthy compact
  new growth, established roots, and suitable drainage matter. A warm room or
  a due watering does not, by itself, prove active growth.
- Pause feeding when growth rests, root health is uncertain, or the plant is
  stressed. Do not increase the concentration to compensate for a skipped feed.
- The first post-repot cycle is complete. All 18 August-14 repots
  (`P01`-`P18`) received plain Beauchamp's RO water with no fertilizer at 4:22 p.m. on August 26. Their Wet setup-2 weights were recorded between 12:14 and 12:20 a.m. on
  August 27, about eight hours later, so retain that interval when comparing
  the first baseline with later 30–60-minute drainage readings.
- Do not add separate Cal-Mag, sulfur, Foliage-Pro, or another fertilizer to
  the same gallon. This MSU formula already supplies calcium, magnesium, and
  micronutrients.
- Water only a dry, ready pot. Fertilizer changes what is in an otherwise due
  watering; it is never a reason to water early.

If an individual cactus has a soft or dark base, a fresh open wound, newly cut
roots, or another possible rot sign, hold that pot out of the group watering
and inspect it first.

## What the MSU dose supplies

Tezula lists this product as 13% nitrogen, 3% phosphate (`P2O5`), 15% potash
(`K2O`), 8% calcium, 2% water-soluble magnesium, and a complete set of listed
micronutrients. The product is explicitly formulated for RO, rain, or tap
water. The current product page directs the user to the supplied usage guide
for mixing and frequency, and describes periodic plain-water flushing as a
way to manage salts. It does not establish this trial's gram dose or a fixed
flush count.

University of Minnesota Extension describes modest cactus nutrient needs and
much less frequent feeding in its general houseplant guidance. The collection's
more frequent, dilute trial is an adaptation for these pots and
conditions, not a published species-specific recommendation. Free drainage and
RO water alone do not establish how much fertilizer a plant needs or retains.

At **0.50 g per US gallon**, the declared analysis supplies approximately:

| Declared nutrient  | Approximate concentration |
| ------------------ | ------------------------: |
| Nitrogen           |                   17 mg/L |
| Phosphate (`P2O5`) |                  4.0 mg/L |
| Potash (`K2O`)     |                   20 mg/L |
| Calcium            |                   11 mg/L |
| Magnesium          |                  2.6 mg/L |

These are calculations from the guaranteed percentages and a 3.785 L US
gallon, not a meter reading. Product density can change how many grams fit in a
teaspoon, which is why the gram scale is the collection standard.

### Change from the previous routine

On November 27, the owner reported using **0.75 g MSU/gal approximately every
third watering**. The previous written plan instead described alternate
feed/plain-water applications. Neither is a measurement of the nutrients
remaining in the root zone.

At equal watering volumes, the new 0.50 g/gal every-watering trial supplies
**twice the total product** of 0.75 g/gal every third watering, or about
**one-third more** than 0.75 g/gal every second watering. Each individual
application is weaker. These comparisons are arithmetic, not proof that the
old routine caused a deficiency or that the new one is sufficient.

Use actual new growth and plant condition to review the trial. There is no
automatic dose increase after a certain number of feeds. Resting plants use
their appropriate plain-water care even if the lights remain on. Healthy
active growth under indoor lights can support continued dilute feeding; a
calendar season alone does not establish activity.

## Plain water and salt management

An ordinary watering should wet the root zone and allow drainage; discard the
runoff. That is different from a larger corrective leaching treatment. Neither
RO water nor a dilute recipe guarantees that salts cannot accumulate.

- **No fixed flushing count:** four or six waterings is not a measured salt
  threshold. Consider fertilizer concentration, existing mix charge, growth,
  drainage, and evidence of buildup together.
- **Use plain RO when feeding is unsuitable:** paused growth, stressed or
  unestablished roots, uncertain fertilizer charge, or suspected salt injury
  justify reassessment at the next otherwise-needed watering.
- **Investigate before a heavy flush:** mineral crust, new tip burn, or poor
  growth can prompt a check, but symptoms can have other causes. A comparable
  EC reading may help; this collection has no validated runoff-EC threshold.
  Do not flush a damp or poorly draining pot simply to satisfy a count.
- **Keep exceptions separate:** leaf-cycle plants need their own watering
  decision, and a terrarium without confirmed drainage is unsuitable for an
  ordinary through-pot leaching procedure.

Tezula supports periodic plain-water flushing as salt management; Nebraska
Extension describes buildup symptoms and deliberate leaching in drained pots.
Neither establishes a universal interval for this collection. Record a
plain-water event only when it actually happens.

## Measuring a batch

Use the 0.01 g pocket scale. The larger 0.5 g-resolution pot scale is not
precise enough for this dose.

| Finished solution | MSU powder at the 0.50 g/gal trial rate |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------: |
| 1 US quart        |                                  0.13 g |
| 1/2 US gallon     |                                  0.25 g |
| 1 US gallon       |                                  0.50 g |
| 2 US gallons      |                                  1.00 g |
| 5 US gallons      |                                  2.50 g |

The five-gallon HDPE jug remains the plain-RO storage jug. Do not turn the
whole jug into fertilizer solution just because the math is convenient.

1. Use a clean, dry weighing cup; tare it on the 0.01 g scale.
2. Weigh the powder. Do not estimate a fraction of the included scoop.
3. Put about three-quarters of the final RO volume in the clean one-gallon
   pitcher.
4. Add the powder and stir until no granules remain. Top up to the final gallon
   mark and stir again.
5. Apply at room temperature in two slow passes a few minutes apart, continuing
   until the entire root ball is wet and solution exits the drainage holes.
6. Drain 30-60 minutes, empty every saucer, and discard any unused solution the
   same day. Rinse the pitcher and weighing cup with plain water.

For the Garden Logger, record `Nutrients: Yes`, product
`Tezula MSU 13-3-15`, amount `0.50 g/US gal`, and the actual observation date.
On a plain-water event, record `Nutrients: No`. A fertilizer entry does not
replace the Water and Wet-weight events.

Check the logger's product and amount before saving. Its shared **usual dose**
is a user-saved preference, separate from this guide. Updating the guide does
not change a saved preference, an open draft, or historical nutrient amounts.
Use **Save as usual dose** only when the displayed product and new dilution
match the intended routine. The nutrient streak describes past Water events;
it is not a feed or flush instruction.
