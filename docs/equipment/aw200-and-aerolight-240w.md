# AW200 + AeroLight 240 W — Side-by-Side Plan

Updated September 11, 2026. The owner reports that the AW400 is sold out and
has ordered the **new AeroLight 240 W and another GrowHub E25**, expected
**Sunday, September 13, 2026**. The existing **AW200 stays**. The two lights
will hang side by side, with separate height adjustment for taller plants
eventually. Receipt, installation, and new settings remain unconfirmed.

This supersedes the [unavailable AW400 selection](./vivosun-aw400.md) and
[canceled AW400SE order](./vivosun-aw400se.md). Reusing the AW200 costs the owner
less than the replacement plan and preserves independent adjustment. It does
not establish uniform light coverage.

Dimensions and weights come from the [AW200 manual][aw200],
[new-generation manual][new-manual], and [240 W product page][new-product].
The [ordered Amazon listing][ordered-light] currently names the new 240 W light,
but its technical details still include older 200 W information. Use the
manufacturer's VSL-AL240 figures for planning and check the delivered nameplate.
The previous AW400 listing's Samsung 301H EVO claim does not establish the new
240 W light's diode specification.

Both manuals list app dimming from 25–100% and off. Keep one E25 per light for
independent settings. Together with the two existing E6 Gen2 fan controllers,
the plan has **four independent E25 paths: three existing, one incoming**.
Each light needs its own AC supply connection. Keep the canopy fans on their
own controllers; no light-to-fan RJ45 chain is part of this plan.

The pair totals **440 W of rated input**, not measured consumption at the
chosen dimming settings. The existing 13.25-hour day is a planning reference;
record both actual schedules, modes, intensities, and plant-tip clearances
after installation. The earlier 18-inch clearance is not a fresh measurement.
No controller settings or acclimation ramp were changed for this update.

The [new-model manual][new-manual] requires the growing space to stay **below
86°F (30°C)** during operation. Recheck room temperature with both fixtures
running; circulation fans do not establish that the room stays below this limit.

## Coverage and Final Plant Positions

Keep the **24 pots on wood in four columns by six rows**, and the **six containers
on the 24-inch round glass table**. The shared succulent planter remains a
square wooden box. The [Table Placement Guide](../layouts/table-placement-research.md)
retains every final recommended position and its individual light need.

A proposed orientation runs both lights' approximately 26-inch long axes
from **top/Mylar to bottom/white wall**, side by side across wood and toward
glass. Their combined short dimensions are about **25.9 inches before a gap**.
Wood plus the near half of glass spans about **28 inches before furniture gaps**.
Those dimensions help plan placement; they do not prove full or even illumination.
Final fixture centers, rotation, and spacing have not been measured.

Prioritize clear exposure for the high-light plants in the middle wooden rows
and near glass half. Keep Ming Thing at the open-left corner and the money tree
on outer glass, then check that those spots receive gentler useful light.
The overlap between fixtures can be stronger than either edge. Different heights
can improve clearance for tall cacti while changing exposure for their neighbors.
Keep the separate hangers adjustable and the fans unobstructed.

The **two-foot room-end return reflector stays out of the plan**. The open view
is part of the setup: **open room left, Diamond Mylar divider top, white wall
bottom, weak north-facing window right**. Window light remains supplementary.

## Independent Ceiling Hooks

The owner corrected the ceiling framing orientation on September 11: the plan
is now **four ceiling hooks, two per fixture**. The AW200 and AeroLight 240 W
will each hang from their own pair, with separate adjustable hangers so each
light can be leveled and raised or lowered independently.

This replaces the earlier RIMLUFE shared-bar and KAROSOM anti-tip-wire proposal.
Those accessories are no longer part of the mounting plan, and the two lights
do not need to be balanced against each other. Use each fixture's manufacturer
hanging points. Installation, final hook positions, and plant-tip clearances
have not yet been recorded.
