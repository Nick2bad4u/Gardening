## Recommended Starting Decision

**Keep 45% / 38% and the current plant positions for now; do not raise either
light just because the controls are below half power.** This is a provisional
starting decision, not confirmation of adequate or safe exposure at every tip.
The photograph cannot provide PPFD or an accurate hanging distance.

If dimming roughly follows rated input, AW200 at 45% and AL240 at 38% would
give about **181 W combined**, while the opposite assignment gives about
**184 W**. The former AW200-only 60% setting corresponds to about **120 W**
under the same simplifying assumption. These are arithmetic comparisons, not
measured power or PPFD; they show why two lower percentages can still be a
substantial increase. Different spectra, efficiency, spacing, and overlap
prevent treating equal watts as equal plant exposure.

Keep the existing day length while checking the new arrangement; increasing
duration and intensity together makes the cause of a response harder to judge.
The exact 13.25-hour program and fade division remain to be confirmed.

The useful next check is small: measure LED-to-growing-tip clearance under
each fixture, then compare light at the central low rosettes, the tallest
cactus tip, Ming Thing, Variegated Moon, tiger jaws, and the money tree's upper
leaves. Use both lights at their normal settings. A suitable quantum meter
can measure PPFD; a consistent lux reading can reveal relative bright and dim
spots, but do not apply an old AW200SE lux conversion to these mixed spectra.

Leave the high-light rosettes and split rock in clear central exposure. Shift
only a plant that is shaded or receiving excessive exposure. Raise the fixture
over taller cacti if their tips are much closer than the low plants, then
recheck the nearby low plants. Do not dim the whole group merely to protect
one tall tip, or assume an outer corner is automatically gentle.

The [new-model manual][new-manual] requires the growing space to stay **below
86°F (30°C)** during operation. Recheck room temperature with both fixtures
running; circulation fans do not establish that the room stays below this limit.
