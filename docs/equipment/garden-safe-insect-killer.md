# Garden Safe Houseplant & Garden Insect Killer₂

A ready-to-use contact spray for exposed pests on houseplants, garden plants,
and home-greenhouse plants. **HG-93214, 32 fl oz:** pyrethrins **0.01%** + canola
oil **1.00%**, EPA **67702-35-8845**.

[Back to pest-control supplies](./pest-control-supplies.md)

## Best fit

- **Exposed mealybugs, aphids, whiteflies, thrips, scale, and spider mites** are
  listed targets. Aim at the pest's actual location, including leaf undersides
  and joints.
- The label also lists fungus gnats, but this foliar application is not a
  prescribed soil drench for larvae. Use the traps to track adult activity and
  choose a separately labeled larval treatment if needed.
- Best considered when there is an identified, reachable infestation.

## How to use

1. **Test a small area first.** Allow several days to check for burning before
   treating the rest of an unfamiliar plant.
2. **Shake well; use as supplied without dilution.**
3. **Wet the plant thoroughly without dripping or runoff.** Turn the pot to
   reach hidden surfaces rather than repeatedly soaking the same side. The
   label caps each application at **20 fl oz per 100 sq ft**.
4. **Let the spray dry before people or pets re-enter.** Keep spray away from
   food surfaces and aquariums. Record the plant, product, and application date
   so follow-up observations have context.

## Recheck and limits

The plant directions allow another treatment **in 1–2 weeks if needed**. Recheck
for living pests rather than automatically spraying every week. The label allows
no more than **10 applications per season**; its normal minimum interval is
**3 days**, with a **24-hour** exception only under extreme pest pressure.

Do not apply **above 90°F** or **within 21 days of sulfur application**. Do not
spray during rain. These are label restrictions, not suggested treatment targets.

## Fit for this collection

For cacti, Lithops, split rock, Peperomia, and oyster plant, start with one small
test area rather than a whole-shelf treatment. As a practical setup choice,
treat away from the close grow lights and let surfaces dry before returning the
plant. A foliar pest treatment should not become an extra watering for a dry-resting
mesemb; keep the spray directed at the plant and avoid soaking its medium.
