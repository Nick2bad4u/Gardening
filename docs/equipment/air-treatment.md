# Grow-room dehumidifier and air purifier

Last updated: 2026-08-14

These two small appliances support the open-table grow area, but neither one is
a plant controller. The manual temperature/RH sensor remains the decision tool,
the two pole-mounted circulation fans provide cross-canopy air movement, and the
normal soak-and-dry watering rule does not change.

## Current equipment

| Device                                                                                                                                   | Verified details                                                                                                                        | Role in this setup                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [TABYIK DH-CS01 dehumidifier](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CMTP3GH8?th=1)                                                                 | Thermoelectric/Peltier unit; one speed; 1,000 mL tank; 41.67 W; 5.9 × 5.9 × 10.1 in; advertised 28 dB; automatic stop around 700–800 mL | On-demand help when the room sensor shows sustained excess humidity |
| [Levoit Core Mini-P](https://levoit.com/products/core-mini-p-air-purifier) ([Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09GTRVJQM?th=1)) | Model LAP-C161-WUS; 7 W; 34 CFM CADR; 6.5 × 6.5 × 10.4 in; 25–44 dB; 360° intake; three-stage particle/carbon filter; ozone-free        | Low-level dust and pollen removal for the room and camera area      |

TABYIK advertises up to **450 mL/day**, but that test is at **86°F and 80% RH**
in a confined space. The open grow area normally targets much cooler and drier
conditions, so actual collection may be a small fraction of that claim. The
advertised 280 ft² coverage is not a reason to expect whole-room humidity
control. This unit has no humidistat, target-RH setting, drain hose, or timer.

Levoit rates the Core Mini-P for 254 ft² at one air change per hour or 53 ft² at
4.8 changes per hour. It removes airborne particles and some odors; it does
**not** lower humidity and does not replace the oscillating canopy fans.

## Starting placement

Start both devices **beyond the second wooden table on the open-room side**,
outside the nominal 60 in plant bay and the grow light's direct footprint. The
24 in round table plus two 16 in table depths nominally uses 56 in before gaps
and leg interference, so the remaining space is not an appliance bay. This
placement keeps the devices away from the window, watering work, camera view,
and strongest paired-fan sweep.

1. Put each appliance on its own rigid, stable board or shallow waterproof tray
   above the loose floor Mylar. The Mylar is a carpet liner, not a basin.
2. Put the **Core Mini-P on the divider side** of the room-end space. Keep its
   360° intake open and its top outlet unobstructed. Do not tuck it against the
   divider fabric or Mylar.
3. Put the **DH-CS01 on the white-wall side**, but rotate it so the rear intake
   faces the open room rather than the wall. Leave at least 6 in behind that
   intake as a conservative starting clearance and keep the top exhaust clear.
4. Leave roughly 8–12 in between the two appliances so one exhaust does not
   feed directly into the other's intake.
5. Keep the temperature/RH sensor at canopy height between plant surfaces. Do not
   place it beside either appliance; intake or exhaust air would make the
   reading less representative of the plants.
6. Keep both adapters, plugs, and cable connections outside watering paths.
   Route each cord with a drip loop before it reaches a floor-level adapter or
   power strip.

These are starting positions based on the measured 36 × approximately 60 in
bay and three end-to-end surfaces. Record the real fit and cord reach before
treating them as final. If either unit narrows the aisle or causes a cable
crossing, move both farther toward the open room rather than squeezing them
beside a table.

## Simple operating plan

| Sensor or room condition                                                                                 | Core Mini-P                                                                            | DH-CS01                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Normal 30–55% RH                                                                                         | Low continuously; lights-on hours only is also fine if the nighttime noise is annoying | Off                                                                                                             |
| Repotting, dusty top dressing, or bringing plants in from outside                                        | High for 30–60 minutes **after cleanup**, then return to low                           | Off unless the humidity rule also triggers                                                                      |
| Brief RH maximum above 60%, but the current reading and 24-hour minimum return below 55%                 | Low                                                                                    | Off; this is a short spike, not sustained humidity                                                              |
| Current RH is above 60% at both a morning and evening check, or the 24-hour RH minimum remains above 60% | Low                                                                                    | Run for 12–24 hours, then check current RH and tank collection                                                  |
| Condensation appears or current RH remains above 65%                                                     | Low                                                                                    | Run while the room is closed, inspect for wet trays or another moisture source, and recheck after several hours |
| Current RH reaches 45–55% after a dehumidifier run                                                       | Low                                                                                    | Turn off and observe the next 24-hour range                                                                     |
| Current RH is below 35% or the 24-hour minimum falls below 30%                                           | Low or off                                                                             | Off                                                                                                             |

Do **not** start the dehumidifier automatically after every watering. Empty the
pot trays, keep normal airflow, and let the sensor show whether the room
actually stayed humid. A short post-watering maximum above 60% is acceptable if
the current reading and 24-hour minimum return to the normal band.

The August 14 emergency repot and added perlite can create fine dust. Run the
Core Mini-P on high only after the work surface is cleaned and all loose medium
has settled, then return it to low. Do not use either canopy fan to blow repotting
dust through the room or into the purifier.

The DH-CS01 works best in warm, humid conditions; the listing gives 59–95°F as
its suitable range and says performance becomes more obvious as humidity rises.
If it runs for 24–48 hours during genuinely high humidity without producing a
useful RH drop, the open room is beyond this small Peltier unit's practical
capacity. Fix the moisture source or use room HVAC/a humidistat-equipped
compressor dehumidifier instead of running the mini unit indefinitely.

Leave the Core Mini-P aroma pad dry. Essential oil is unrelated to plant or
particle care and would add an unnecessary variable beside the collection.

## August 4 commissioning check

1. With both new appliances off for at least an hour, record current
   temperature/RH and the 24-hour ranges.
2. Start the Core Mini-P on low. Confirm its outlet does not move plant bodies,
   top dressing, labels, Mylar, or the camera cable.
3. Function-test the DH-CS01 power button and indicator. Do not pour water into
   the tank to simulate a full condition; verify the automatic-stop behavior
   naturally if a real humidity run eventually fills it. Leave it off after the
   short test unless the humidity table above calls for a run.
4. After 24 hours, record the same sensor values. If the dehumidifier ran, also
   record its runtime and approximate water collected.
5. After 72 hours, confirm neither adapter is unusually hot, both intakes are
   clean, the aisle remains clear, and no cord can enter a watering path.

This short comparison is enough. There is no need to build a detailed air-
quality or humidity experiment for a hobby collection.

## Maintenance and safety

- Empty and dry the dehumidifier tank after each run. Its automatic shutoff
  prevents overflow from a full tank; it does not protect the appliance from a
  watering spill.
- Discard collected condensate. Do not use it as cactus water; it can contain
  dust, microbes, and residues from the appliance.
- Check the dehumidifier's rear intake and top outlet monthly during use and
  remove visible dust with the unit unplugged.
- Inspect the Core Mini-P prefilter monthly in this soil-and-top-dressing area.
  Follow its check-filter indicator and manual for replacement. Do not wash the
  filter media unless Levoit explicitly permits it.
- Unplug either device before moving it, removing the water tank, cleaning an
  intake, or working on nearby cords.
- Keep at least one clear walking path out of the grow corner. Do not trade
  electrical safety for a few inches of theoretical air treatment.
