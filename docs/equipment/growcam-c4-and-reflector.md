## Recommended physical layout

### Mylar

The first reflector is installed top-to-bottom on the plant-facing side of the
black divider. Leave the white right wall uncovered because it already provides
broad, diffuse reflection.

1. Hang the film flat and taut from the divider's rigid frame, not loose where
   either pole fan can make it flap.
2. Keep the installed full-height sheet clear of the lamp, driver, and open top.
3. Leave the window side and room-facing side open for access, heat escape, and
   airflow.
4. Keep the film clear of fan blades, outlets, adapters, and wet trays.
5. Install it before the final baseline lux or PAR grid. Record readings with
   the film in place because it changes edge intensity.

Keep the white wall uncovered and the room-facing end open. The broader light
replaces the return-panel approach; enjoying and reaching the plants takes
priority over enclosing more sides. No additional reflector is proposed.

### GrowCam

The earlier three-surface plan proposed a rigid point on the right wall, slightly
toward the room side of the second wooden table, aimed diagonally down and
lengthwise toward the round table and Mylar-lined divider.

This direction keeps the bright window out of the center of the image, uses the
divider as a cleaner background, and avoids mounting the camera on fabric that
can move. Do not attach it to either fan, the light, adjustable light hangers, or
anything that vibrates.

That earlier plan used the following starting geometry; these are not current measured camera positions:

- lens roughly 18–30 in above the tallest plant tip;
- camera roughly 24–40 in from the center of the occupied footprint;
- a 30–45° downward view that includes every pot, its short plant ID, and
  several inches of growth space above the tallest plant;
- the light itself and the brightest Mylar reflection kept near or outside the
  image edge; and
- enough clearance from the grow lights to follow VIVOSUN's instruction to install
  the camera away from grow lights to avoid overheating.

Aim tightly enough that the couch, clothing area, and normal room traffic are
outside the frame. Disable audio recording in the app if that option exists and
plant audio is not wanted. Although the hardware is listed as IP54, the manual
calls it a dry indoor device; keep it and its 5 V connection outside watering
and spray paths.

Mark the mount position and angle so framing can be repeated. Start a new clip whenever a table or camera moves enough to change the scene, then keep the camera fixed for that clip.

### Paired fans and electrical equipment

Both pole fans remain separate from the camera:

- keep both rigid divider-pole mounts clear of the fabric and Mylar;
- offset the fan bodies, intakes, cords, and arcs so they cannot collide, and use the tissue test from
  the [operating schedule](./aw200se-operating-schedule.md#paired-pole-fan-placement-and-airflow-test);
- make sure neither sweep can strike the camera cable, the other fan, or the Mylar; and
- raise the power strip, adapters, and all controllers off the floor and
  outside the watering path, with drip loops below every connection.

## Time-lapse program

### Full-day validation test

The full-day check was completed by September 20. The following settings remain a useful commissioning reference after changing a camera, card or recording setup; the exact options used in the completed test were not recorded.

| Setting              | Starting value                                                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Interval             | 5 minutes                                                                |
| Duration             | 24 hours                                                                 |
| Framing              | Include every plant, the light-on period, and a full dark period         |
| Continuous recording | Off if the current app provides a reliable independent toggle            |
| Check                | Confirm solid green before starting and slow green flashing while active |

Download and play the result before relying on a multiweek recording. Confirm
that focus is acceptable, the window does not dominate exposure, light-on and
light-off transitions work, timestamps are correct, and the file can be
exported from the current app version.

### Main acclimation time-lapse

The original six-week recording plan covered August 3–September 14. The August
8–9 table expansion and August 14 repot, second fan and light change marked
useful boundaries for separate clips. The table preserves that dated plan.

| Setting              | Starting value                                                                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Start                | August 3, 2026, by 7:45 a.m.                                                                 |
| End                  | September 14, 2026, after the light turns off                                                |
| Interval             | 15 minutes                                                                                   |
| Camera position      | Fixed for the entire clip                                                                    |
| Daily capture window | 8:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. if the current app offers one; otherwise allow 24-hour day/night capture |
| Weekly rotation      | Keep the Sunday 90° pot rotations; the visible change is expected                            |
| Verification         | Preview after 24 hours and export a test after the first week                                |

At a conventional 30 frames per second, a 15-minute interval over six weeks is
roughly a two-minute result if the camera captures all 24 hours, or roughly one
minute if it captures only the 12-hour light window. The VIVOSUN app determines
the actual output frame rate and estimated duration, so use its displayed
estimate as the final check.

For a quieter long-term record after September 14, a 30- or 60-minute interval is
enough for slow cactus growth. Start a fresh clip whenever the seasonal layout,
camera angle, or table changes materially.

The official manual documents the hardware, card requirement, and recording
indicator but does not document every time-lapse interval, daily-window, export,
or storage-management option in the changing app. Check the controls available in the installed app before planning a recording.

## Final checks

Use this checklist when setting up or reframing a recording:

- camera is on a rigid mount and outside the lamp's heat path;
- all pots and short position labels are visible while the couch and normal
  room traffic are excluded;
- the window is not the dominant background;
- Mylar is taut and cannot enter the fan sweep;
- a post-Mylar lux or PAR grid has been recorded;
- both fans reach their assigned canopy zones without moving the camera,
  reflector, or each other;
- power equipment is raised outside the watering path; and
- the app recognizes the installed card and can export a playable test.

Keep original recordings backed up separately from the camera card; export selected clips for viewing.
