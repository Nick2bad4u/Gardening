# VIVOSUN AeroLight Wing AW400SE

Updated September 10, 2026. The owner has **ordered the AW400SE, the fixed
full-spectrum model**, to replace the tunable-spectrum AW200. Delivery is
expected Sunday, according to the owner; the delivery date and installation
have not been confirmed. This page records the intended setup after it arrives.

The aim is a brighter, more even display that remains enjoyable to look at.
**Remove the approximately two-foot Mylar return panel at the room-facing end.**
Keep the existing Mylar on the divider, the white wall, and the current tables.
The new plan leaves the view and access from the room open.

## Exact Model and Manufacturer Specifications

VIVOSUN's [AeroLight SE manual][manual] includes **VSAW400SE** and
VSAW400SE-E25. Its specifications support the owner's approximately
26 × 26-inch description:

| Item                   | Manufacturer information                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixture dimensions     | **26 × 26.4 × 2.2 inches** / 66 × 67 × 5.6 cm                                                                          |
| Weight                 | **12.5 lb / 5.69 kg**                                                                                                  |
| Rated input power      | **400 W**                                                                                                              |
| Spectrum               | Fixed full spectrum, with white, 660 nm deep-red, and 730 nm far-red LEDs; no tunable growth-stage spectrum is planned |
| Panels                 | Two angle-adjustable wings; the collection plan uses them fully flat                                                   |
| Published growing area | **4 × 4 ft tent** in the manufacturer's application table                                                              |
| Control                | GrowHub E25 through Type-C; compatible E42-series controllers are also listed                                          |
| Published dimming      | App: 25–100% and off; controller presets: 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, off                                                     |

The footprint is roughly twice the panel area of the manual's 26 × 13-inch
200 W SE reference. That is a model comparison, not a measurement of the
installed tunable AW200 or a promise of twice the light at every plant.

## Placement With the Room End Open

Use the [Table Placement Guide](../layouts/table-placement-research.md) for
the 30 final recommended pot positions and the illustrated light-need groups.
In its bird's-eye view:

- **Left:** open room-facing end; the two-foot return panel comes out.
- **Top:** the existing Diamond Mylar divider stays.
- **Bottom:** the uncovered white wall stays.
- **Right:** the north-facing window beyond the round glass table; the owner
  reports weak supplementary daylight.

The intended broad light area covers **all 24 wooden-table pots and roughly
the nearer half of the glass table**. The two wooden tops form a nominal
16 × 26-inch rectangle, with four columns and six rows. The glass table remains
24 inches across, with the square wooden shared succulent box and five other
containers. No replacement table or new reflector is part of this setup.

The wood plus half the glass diameter spans about **28 inches before gaps**.
The fixture itself is about 26 inches across, so its physical outline is not
the boundary of useful light. Light spreading beyond the panels makes the
intended coverage plausible, but the advertised tent area does not verify
uniform exposure in this open room. Illustrations show the intended growing
area schematically; they are not measured PPFD maps.

Keep high-light plants in clear lamp exposure, softer-light plants toward the
perimeter, and the money tree on the outer glass side. Ming Thing moves to
the wooden grid's open-left corner, **R1C1**, as the candidate for gentler
exposure. That corner is still under the broader light; its position alone
does not establish bright indirect conditions. The plant's ongoing need stays I.
