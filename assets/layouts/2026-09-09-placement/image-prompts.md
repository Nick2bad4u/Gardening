specifies Samsung 301H EVO diodes, 395 nm UVA, and 730 nm far-red. Equipment

room end remains open; the top Mylar divider, bottom white wall, and weak north
window at right remain. Delivery, installation, intensity, and spectrum mode
are unconfirmed. The art shows planned coverage, not measured brightness.

Generated with the built-in image tool from the preceding committed versions
of these PNGs. The botanical drawings are schematic illustrations; use the
[placement research](../../../docs/layouts/table-placement-research.md) for
