# AW200 + AeroLight 240 W Placement Illustrations

Created September 11, 2026, using the built-in image-generation tool to edit the
[September 10 AW400 illustrations](../2026-09-09-placement/image-prompts.md).
The older PNGs remain in that directory as historical proposals. These three
images are the current assets for the placement document, layout page, and
generated booklet.

The AW400 is unavailable. The owner will keep the AW200 and add the new
**VSL-AL240 AeroLight 240 W**, with another E25 expected September 13. The
[equipment note](../../../docs/equipment/aw200-and-aerolight-240w.md) records exact
dimensions, weights, source distinctions, and the planned independent suspension
from four ceiling hooks (two per light). The illustrations do not show mounting
hardware, so the revised hook plan does not change these images.
The light silhouettes indicate a proposed orientation; they do not establish
fixture centers, measured light levels, or a completed installation.

## Shared Edit Requirements

- Preserve the 24 wooden pots in **four columns × six rows**, with all existing
  plant names, IDs, and need badges. No rearrangement or new acclimation stages.
- Preserve all six glass containers, including **#2 as a square wooden box**,
  on the **24-inch round glass table**. Illustrations are not scale drawings
  or new measurements of pot rims.
- Keep wood left, glass right, **open room left**, **Diamond Mylar top**,
  **white wall bottom**, and the **weak north-facing window right**. No left
  return reflector.
- Retain H = High light, B = Bright filtered light, I = Bright indirect light,
  H/B = Mixed needs in one shared pot. These are horticultural needs, not
  measured exposure at a position.
- Replace all active AW400 text and single-fixture dimensions with the two-light
  plan. Keep unknown installation settings out of the images.
