# Original nursery labels

This folder preserves supplier labels from the collection. `nursery-labels` is
more accurate than the original `Nursery-Cards` name: some evidence is a
printed sleeve, a sticker, or only one side of a label. The starter-label
photos were captured on 2026-07-23 and renamed by the plant name and visible
label face.

A nursery label is useful provenance, but it is not automatically a correct
botanical identification. The original “Opuntia Cylindrica Cristata” sleeve is
retained under a filename that records the likely Ming Thing mislabel.

## August additions

The six August cacti and Kiwi aeonium came from Carlsons' Greenhouse on Torrey
Road; the money tree came from Lowe's. The `E1`–`F3` and `#1`–`#4` mappings were
copied from the physical labels on 2026-08-10. The two Pachira files were
created on 2026-08-26 as clean, AI-assisted presentation crops from photos
supplied in chat; they are not untouched camera exports. They preserve the
visible genus-level tag and LiveTrends branding but do not prove a species.
